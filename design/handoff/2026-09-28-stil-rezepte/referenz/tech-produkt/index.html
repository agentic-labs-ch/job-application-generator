Dear Hiring Team,

I have seen Azure adoption from both sides of the table. At Globex Consulting I helped deliver a global migration of more than 1,000 servers to Azure; at Beispiel Kantonalbank, as an ICT architect, I advised the CIO boards that decided, funded and governed the move to the cloud. As a Customer Success Account Manager, I want to bring both perspectives to your customers.

Swiss enterprises in regulated industries decide on Azure the way Beispiel Kantonalbank did: through architecture reviews, business cases and CIO boards. I know that path from the inside, and I would like to help Nimbus Software's customers go down it faster, from cloud adoption to AI.

What I bring:

  • Trusted advice at board level: I have advised CIO boards and know what they weigh, from business priorities to procurement. I would use this to tie each customer's technology investments to the goals their leadership has set.
  • Success plans with measurable value: as an architect I backed cloud decisions with business cases and dashboards. I would build Customer Success Plans the same way, with shared objectives and success measures that the customer's governance can track.
  • Risks and dependencies: Azure migrations at Globex Consulting showed me where adoption stalls, from identity to operating models. I would raise these risks early and agree with the customer and the account team on who owns them.
  • Nimbus depth: the Azure Solutions Architect certification (AZ-305), and I know the Azure service catalogue from the customer's point of view.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I would lead Customer Success Planning for your customers in Switzerland.

Kind regards
Alex Muster
